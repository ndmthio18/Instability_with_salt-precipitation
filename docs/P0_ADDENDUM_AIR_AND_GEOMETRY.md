# P0 addendum: air closure and surface-growth geometry

This addendum is separate from, and does not revise, the frozen P0 GO/NO-GO conclusions.

## Air-component closure at a future soil--crust interface

P1 conserves H2O, air and NaCl in a two-phase soil. A future saturated crust cannot therefore be coupled with only water and salt conditions while silently deleting the soil air balance. P3 must state whether air is blocked, displaced laterally, dissolved, stored below the crust, or transported through connected gas pathways. The interface condition must close the air-component flux and any interfacial air storage. This closure is not implemented in P1.

## Normal growth versus vertical height

If a future crust surface is represented by a height function \(h(\mathbf x_\parallel,t)\) and Prat supplies a normal growth speed \(v_n\), the vertical height rate is

\[
\partial_t h=v_n\sqrt{1+|\nabla_\parallel h|^2}.
\]

Thus \(v_n\) cannot be inserted directly as \(\partial_t h\) once the surface is non-planar. P1 contains no surface state, moving mesh, precipitation or geometric growth.
