The exploratory 4T results have been reviewed.

The large perturbation growth is scientifically interesting, but late-time mode selection is currently underresolved. The unseeded M0 simulation also develops substantial two-dimensional structure.

Do not launch an unchanged R24 8T simulation.

## Task 1 — Develop the controlled R48 validation

Prepare a Case 36877 canonical nx28 single-wavelength R48 model:

- Same physical wavelength W=0.6/28 m
- 48 horizontal cells and 200 vertical cells
- Same A28 eigenprofile
- M1 amplitude 1e-6
- M0 amplitude 0
- Same seven observation planes
- Same dt/2 time integration
- Same physical and constitutive parameters

Before running to 4T, perform static equivalence, initial inventory, actual cut-coordinate, grid-resolution and bounded runtime checks.

Define the validation metrics and stopping criteria before viewing the new results.

If resources and the registered cost limits permit, execute one bounded R48 M0/M1 pair to 4T. If the projected cost exceeds the allowed budget, stop after a short benchmark and report the necessary budget.

Preserve all R24 historical results.

## Task 2 — Test whether late-time wavelengths converge

Compare R24 and R48 at identical physical times, especially from 3T to 4T.

Quantify:

- Growth of weighted sigma, Fourier and eigenmode projection;
- Dominant physical wavenumber and wavelength;
- Mode energy fractions;
- Odd Neumann modes and reflection symmetry;
- Depth-resolved concentration perturbations;
- Local maximum concentration;
- Seven-plane signed salt flux and cumulative excess transport;
- Background M0 perturbation growth.

The central question is:

Does the dominant late-time mode remain at the same physical wavelength when horizontal resolution is increased?

Do not confuse a higher mode number with a finer physical wavelength when domains differ.

If dominant modes remain close to the numerical resolution limit, or change substantially with refinement, classify physical late-time convection as unresolved and propose R96 only after measuring cost.

## Task 3 — Establish an independent one-dimensional background

Design and verify a 1D background simulation using the same physical equations, water retention/relative permeability model, evaporation boundary, bottom boundary and vertical discretization.

Compare early-time 1D profiles and water/salt budgets against the initially uniform 2D M0 solution.

Do not assume the two discretizations are identical without verification.

Retain three distinct references:

1. 1D background with no lateral convection;
2. Unseeded 2D M0;
3. Seeded 2D M1.

Use these to separate background salt accumulation from the influence of seeded and unseeded lateral instability.

## Task 4 — Plan spatially resolved conservative transport diagnostics

Audit whether the current SCVF transport kernel can expose horizontal distributions of vertical water and salt fluxes at selected depths.

Prepare diagnostics for:

- Local upward and downward advective salt flux;
- Lateral fraction occupied by downward flow;
- Concentration–vertical-flow covariance;
- Spatial association between high-salinity regions and downward velocity;
- Penetration of actual transport structures with time.

Keep horizontally integrated fluxes and the existing seven-plane conservation identities unchanged.

Do not substitute reconstructed VTK velocities for quantitative conservative fluxes without validation.

## Task 5 — Reassess the physical conclusion

After the validation, distinguish:

- Numerically observed perturbation amplification;
- Grid-resolved short-wave development;
- Verified downward local convective transport;
- Changes in mean and maximum surface concentration;
- Actual solubility-threshold events.

Keep the original failed numerical screens in the audit trail.

Do not assign t_eff or a crystallization-delay label until the required physical and numerical evidence is available.

Provide a final Chinese review with GO/NO-GO for R96, multiwavelength validation and any later 8T simulations. Commit reproducible reports and evidence to the same branch.

Do not automatically launch 8T or a large multi-case campaign.
