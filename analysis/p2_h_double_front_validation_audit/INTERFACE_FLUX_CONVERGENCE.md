# Early interface flux convergence

Protocol (fixed before analysis): compare both F+ and F− in kg m−2 s−1 and relative to Sref=Ceq j/(1−Ceq); require every spatial pair (128/256 and 256/512 crust cells, liquid cells n/4) and every temporal pair to differ by <1% of Sref at all subsequent common saved times through 100 s. The earliest qualifying sampled time is reported. It does not prove a continuous-in-time bound between samples. A failed member prevents certification of its full requested interval, regardless of its accepted-prefix agreement.

Three initial liquid thicknesses (0.1, 1, 10 nm) are distinct physical initial conditions. Their differences are not included as mesh-convergence error. The liquid mesh is already uniform; there is no liquid stretching to remove. The crust has cosine clustering at both interfaces.

The uniform initial condition is C=Ceq in both regions, with nonzero imposed bottom diffusion. Boundary concentrations and moving-interface roots are solved algebraically, but the initial bulk field is not a steady response to that forcing. Therefore an actual early relaxation exists. Outputs span 1e-10 s to 100 s, with explicit first-second panels. Internal maximum steps are 1e-8, 1e-6, 1e-4, .001, .01 and .1 s over progressively longer early windows. The refined test halves those caps and tightens rtol from 1e-9 to 1e-10. Internal accepted steps are recorded; output spacing alone is never used as evidence of integration accuracy.

Alternative consistent initialization solves dc_i/dt=0 at the same prescribed initial geometry and solid inventory, using the same interface roots and instantaneous geometry/pore-volume derivatives. With e=C−Ceq, the residual is Tdot_i−rho_l e_i Edot_i. Newton uses independently differentiated residuals and a dense pivoted solve; it must reach max residual <1e-15 kg m−2 s−1. The alternative has a different initial dissolved inventory, which is explicitly budgeted from its own initial state. It is not spliced into the uniform trajectory and does not remove the uniform run's physical transient.

All initial-thickness and initialization comparisons retain their exact physical initial state and forcing. Original failed numerical settings are archived, and improved retries receive new case IDs. `results/flux_windows.json` records earliest supported windows; spatial/time tables retain absolute errors and common endpoints. No first-hour exclusion is imposed.
